# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis using SQL

### 🎯 Project Objective
Analyzed Superstore sales dataset to identify key business trends, find loss-making categories, and provide data-driven recommendations to increase profitability by 15%+.

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- **SQL**: Data cleaning, Joins, CTEs, Window Functions
- **Excel**: Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP, Charts
- **Dataset**: Superstore Sales (9,900+ rows)

### 🔍 Key Business Questions Solved
1. Which product categories have the highest profit margins?
2. Which regions are underperforming and why?
3. What is the monthly sales trend for 2022-2023?
4. Who are the top 10 most profitable customers?

### 📈 Key Insights & Findings
- **Technology** category drives 45% of total profit despite only 30% of sales.
- **Furniture** has negative profit in the South region due to high shipping costs.
- **Q4 sales** contribute 38% of annual revenue. Peak season identified.
- **Top 5 customers** contribute 12% of total profit. High retention opportunity.

### 💡 Business Recommendations
1. **Increase marketing spend** on Technology products in Q4 to maximize ROI.
2. **Renegotiate shipping contracts** for Furniture in South region OR increase prices by 8%.
3. **Launch loyalty program** for top 10 customers to reduce churn risk.
